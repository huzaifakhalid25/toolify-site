Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Toolify ("we", "us") handles when you use our website and free online tools, and what choices you have.

1. The Short Version

  • You do not need an account to use Toolify.
  • Most tools work entirely in your browser. Files, images, PDFs and text you use in those tools are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information You Give Us

We do not ask you to register. If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we receive the details you choose to send (such as your name, email address and message). We use them only to reply to you.

3. Files and Text You Use in Tools

When you use tools such as PDF, image, text or calculator tools, the processing happens locally in your browser. We cannot see your files or text. Some tools save small amounts of data on your own device so that they remember your work, for example the Online Notepad and the Tasbih Counter use your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.

4. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of the visit in server logs. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and understand general usage. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

5. Third-Party Services

To work properly, our pages load resources from trusted third parties. These providers may receive technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests those resources:

  • Content delivery networks for design and code libraries (for example Tailwind CSS, Font Awesome, jsPDF, pdf-lib, pdf.js, JSZip, docx and marked).
  • The QR Code Generator tool uses the api.qrserver.com service, so the text you enter for a QR code is sent to that service to create the image.
  • The YouTube Thumbnail tool loads thumbnail images directly from YouTube.
  • The Speech to Text tool uses your browser's speech recognition feature. Depending on your browser (for example Chrome), audio may be processed by the browser vendor's speech service.

6. Cookies and Advertising

Toolify may display advertisements from Google AdSense in the future. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more at aboutads.info. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using cookies for advertising. If we add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy first.

7. Children's Privacy

Toolify is a general audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can use Toolify without giving us any personal information. You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion of messages you sent us.

9. Security

We use secure HTTPS connections. Because most processing happens on your device, your files are never sent to us. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, so please avoid entering highly sensitive information into any online tool unless you are comfortable doing so.

10. Links to Other Websites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of Toolify after changes means you accept the updated policy.
